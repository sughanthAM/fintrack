About FinTrack

FinTrack is a comprehensive personal finance management web application designed to help individuals take control of their financial life.

Whether you're a student managing a tight budget, a professional tracking monthly expenses, or someone planning for long-term financial goals — FinTrack provides the tools and insights you need to make smarter decisions.

Built with modern web technologies including HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, PHP, and MySQL, FinTrack offers a responsive, intuitive experience across all devices.
